Airport pickup guide

What to Know Before Collecting Your Car

Casablanca Mohammed V International Airport (CMN) is an arrival point for travellers visiting Casablanca and other parts of Morocco. Check your rental confirmation before travelling so you know how to find your provider and collect the vehicle.

Collection procedures vary. Your provider may use an airport desk, a meeting point or shuttle arrangements. Always follow the instructions for your particular booking.

  • Save your booking confirmation and rental company's contact details.
  • Check the exact pickup point and any shuttle or meeting instructions.
  • Bring the driving licence and identification documents requested by the provider.
  • Confirm the payment card requirements, security deposit and excess.
  • Photograph existing damage and the fuel level before driving away.
  • Keep your rental agreement and return instructions accessible.
Use your booking confirmation to verify the exact collection location, documents, contact details and return procedure.

Before you book

  • Compare the total cost, including mandatory fees.
  • Check the security deposit and excess.
  • Read insurance coverage and exclusions carefully.
  • Check the fuel policy and mileage limits.
  • Review additional-driver and young-driver fees if relevant.
  • Understand cancellation and late-return conditions.

Useful answers

Casablanca Airport Car Rental FAQs

Important points to check before arranging your rental.

Where do I collect a rental car at Casablanca Airport?

The collection point depends on your rental company and booking. Check your confirmation for desk, meeting point or shuttle instructions.

How can I compare car rental prices at CMN?

Enter your dates and compare suitable vehicles. Check the full price and terms, including deposit, insurance, fuel, mileage and additional fees.

Do I need a credit card to rent a car?

Requirements vary by provider and vehicle. Some rentals require a credit card in the main driver's name for the security deposit. Confirm the accepted card type before booking.

What documents should I bring?

Check your provider's requirements for your driving licence, passport or identification, booking confirmation and payment card. Confirm whether an international driving permit is required for your licence.

Is insurance included in the rental price?

Coverage differs between offers. Read what is included, the excess, exclusions and any optional cover. Do not assume all types of damage are covered.

Can I drive from Casablanca to other Moroccan cities?

Check that your rental agreement permits your intended journey and review any mileage limits or restrictions. Plan your route and allow enough time.

Will booking through AtlasDrive Morocco cost extra?

AtlasDrive Morocco may earn an affiliate commission from qualifying bookings. Review the price and conditions displayed by the booking provider before confirming.
