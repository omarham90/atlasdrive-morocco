Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

AtlasDrive Morocco ("we", "us" or "our") is a car rental comparison website focused on airport destinations in Morocco. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our website.

Current website status: AtlasDrive Morocco is currently a prototype. Its car listings and prices are examples, and it does not currently accept reservations or payments directly.

1. Information we collect

We do not currently ask visitors to create accounts or submit payment information. The search form allows you to select an airport, travel dates and a driver's age. These selections are used by the website's demonstration interface and are not currently sent to a rental booking system by our code.

Our website hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical information associated with requests to serve the website, such as information in server logs. Please consult GitHub's own privacy information for details about its processing.

2. How we use information

We may use information to operate, maintain, secure and improve the website. If we introduce analytics, contact forms or other services, this policy will be updated to explain their use.

3. Affiliate links and bookings

We intend to participate in car rental affiliate programmes. If approved, some links or booking widgets may direct you to third-party rental providers. We may earn a commission when a qualifying booking is made through an eligible affiliate link, at no additional cost to you where the programme's terms provide for this.

When you visit a third-party provider, that provider's privacy policy and terms apply to information it collects. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party websites.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not currently include its own analytics or advertising cookies. Hosting services and future third-party widgets may use cookies or similar technologies. If we add services that require consent, we will provide appropriate information and controls before using them.

5. Data sharing

We do not currently sell personal information. If affiliate services, analytics, support tools or other integrations are added, information may be processed by those providers as described in an updated policy.

6. Data retention and security

We aim to use appropriate measures to protect information. No website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Retention periods will depend on the information and services involved.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal information. You may also have rights to object to certain processing or complain to a relevant data protection authority.

8. Children's privacy

This website is intended for people planning car rentals and is not designed specifically for children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or its services change. The date at the top of this page will indicate when it was last revised.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the website operator through an appropriate contact address once one has been established.

This policy is a starting point for the current prototype, not legal advice. It should be reviewed and completed before adding third-party tracking, accepting customer information or launching live bookings.
