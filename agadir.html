Before collecting your rental car

  • Confirm the pickup location, desk hours and any shuttle arrangements.
  • Check the driving licence, identification and documents required by the provider.
  • Review the deposit, insurance excess, exclusions and payment-card rules.
  • Photograph the car and record existing damage before driving away.
  • Confirm the fuel policy, mileage allowance and return instructions.
