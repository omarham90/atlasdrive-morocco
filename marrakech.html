Airport pickup guide

What to Know Before Collecting Your Car

Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK) serves travellers visiting the Red City and surrounding destinations. Before travelling, check your booking confirmation carefully so you know where and how your rental company will hand over the vehicle.

Collection arrangements differ between providers. Some may direct customers to a desk, a meeting point or a shuttle. Do not assume every company collects customers in the same place.

  • Save your booking confirmation and the rental company's contact details.
  • Check whether pickup instructions require you to meet a representative or use a shuttle.
  • Bring the driving licence and identification documents requested by the rental company.
  • Check the payment card rules, deposit and excess before you arrive.
  • Photograph the vehicle's existing damage and its fuel level at collection.
  • Keep a copy of the rental agreement and return instructions.
Your booking confirmation is the best source for the exact collection point, opening arrangements, documents and contact details for your rental.

Before you book

  • Compare the total price, not just the advertised daily rate.
  • Review the security deposit and excess.
  • Check what insurance is included and what remains your responsibility.
  • Read the fuel policy and mileage limits.
  • Confirm additional-driver and young-driver fees, if applicable.
  • Check cancellation and late-return conditions.

Practical Driving Tips for Marrakech

A little preparation can make driving in an unfamiliar city easier.

🅿️

Plan Your Parking

Some streets in and around the old medina are narrow or restricted. Ask your accommodation about suitable parking before arrival.

🛣️

Allow Extra Time

Traffic, road layouts and unfamiliar junctions can slow your journey. Plan your route and avoid rushing to meet a pickup or return time.

📍

Check Your Route

Use a current navigation service, follow local road signs and confirm that your planned route is suitable for your rental vehicle.

Marrakech Airport Car Rental FAQs

Important points to check before arranging your rental.

Where do I collect my rental car at Marrakech Airport?

The collection point depends on the rental company and booking. Check your confirmation for the exact desk, meeting point, shuttle instructions or other collection arrangements before travelling.

How can I compare car rental prices at RAK?

Enter your travel dates in the comparison tool and compare suitable vehicles. Check the final price and terms, including any deposit, insurance, fuel, mileage and additional fees.

Do I need a credit card to rent a car in Marrakech?

Requirements vary by rental company and vehicle. Some bookings require a credit card in the main driver's name for the security deposit. Confirm the accepted card type and deposit requirements before booking.

What documents should I bring?

Check the rental company's requirements for your driving licence, passport or identification, booking confirmation and payment card. International driving permit requirements can depend on your licence and the rental company's rules, so confirm these in advance.

Is insurance included in the rental price?

Coverage varies by offer. Read what is included, the excess you may have to pay, exclusions and any optional cover. Do not assume that every type of damage is covered.

Is a small car suitable for Marrakech?

A small car may be convenient for many city journeys and easier to park in some places. Consider passenger numbers, luggage, your route and the parking arrangements at your accommodation.

Can I drive from Marrakech to other Moroccan cities?

Check that your rental agreement permits your intended journey and review any mileage restrictions or other conditions. Plan your route and allow enough time for the distance.
