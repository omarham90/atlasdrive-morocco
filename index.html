Need to know

Frequently asked questions

Are the displayed prices real?

No. The current cars and prices are examples. Real offers require an approved rental partner and a live integration.

Can I collect a car at the airport?

Airport collection depends on the vehicle, rental company and availability shown by the eventual booking partner.

How will AtlasDrive Morocco earn money?

The planned business model is affiliate marketing. If a visitor books through an approved partner link and the booking qualifies, AtlasDrive Morocco may receive a commission.

Does this website make a reservation?

No. This prototype does not process bookings or payments. A real booking must be completed through an integrated rental partner.
