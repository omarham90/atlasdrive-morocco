Plan before you arrive

What to Check Before Renting a Car in Morocco

Comparing the headline price is only the first step. The right rental also depends on the deposit, insurance terms, payment card rules and collection arrangements.

Check your booking confirmation carefully. Different rental companies may use airport desks, meeting points, shuttle services or other pickup arrangements.

  • Compare the total price for your dates, not just the daily rate.
  • Check the security deposit and excess before booking.
  • Review what insurance is included and what is excluded.
  • Confirm accepted payment cards and driver-document requirements.
  • Check the fuel policy, mileage rules and additional fees.
  • Photograph existing vehicle damage when collecting your car.

Make an informed choice

Your needs will depend on your route, passengers and luggage. Before choosing a vehicle, consider:

  • Small cars for many city journeys.
  • More space for families and extra luggage.
  • Comfort for longer road trips.
  • Parking arrangements at your accommodation.
  • Any restrictions in the rental agreement.

Useful answers

Morocco Airport Car Rental FAQs

Some important questions to consider before you book.

Which Moroccan airports can I compare?

AtlasDrive Morocco has airport guides for Marrakech (RAK), Casablanca (CMN), Agadir (AGA), Rabat (RBA), Tangier (TNG) and Fes (FEZ).

How do I compare car rental prices?

Choose your airport, enter your travel dates and compare suitable options. Review the total price and rental terms before confirming a booking.

Do I need a credit card to rent a car in Morocco?

Requirements vary by rental company and vehicle. Some rentals require a credit card in the main driver's name for the security deposit. Confirm the accepted card type before booking.

Is insurance included in the rental price?

Coverage varies between offers. Check the included protection, excess, exclusions and optional cover rather than assuming all damage is covered.

Where do I collect the car at the airport?

Collection arrangements depend on the rental company. Check your booking confirmation for the desk, meeting point, shuttle or other pickup instructions.
